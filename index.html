About the Club

The AI Club is a student-focused space for learning, experimentation, creativity, collaboration, and technology at Cantonment English School And College. It exists for students who are curious about how intelligent systems work — and want to actually build them.

From first-time coders to students already tinkering with their own models, the club meets everyone where they are and pushes them a little further.

"

The AI Club aims to make Artificial Intelligence accessible and exciting for students by giving them opportunities to learn, experiment, build, compete, and share knowledge.
